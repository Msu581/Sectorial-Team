01 Overview

Six verification tools for examination records.

The suite brings together the checks the examination office runs on gradesheets: comparing issued PDFs against the tabulated result, comparing white-background copies against sealed originals, re-deriving the numbers printed on each sheet, auditing the consolidated marksheet, building the provisional result and checking semester eligibility and re-checking improvement and supplementary marks. Each tool runs on its own and produces an Excel report you can download.

02 Tools

Choose a verification tool

The six tools are separate applications. Opening one does not affect the others, and you can come back here to switch at any time.

01

Gradesheet ↔ tabulated result

Marksheet vs TR Verification

Loads the TR spreadsheet and a batch of gradesheet PDFs, pairs them on registration number, and reports every field that does not agree — identity block, subject table, credit structure, marks and totals — in one Excel workbook.

  • TR → PDF
  • Match
  • Course codes
  • Marks
  • Credits
  • Excel report
  • Reads the TR from a chosen worksheet; TR layout is auto-detected, with a manual override for the three known formats.
  • Handles the transcript identity formats in use, including sheets without an ABC ID row, which are reported as N/A rather than blank.
  • Checks subject rows by course code, falling back to course name, and flags duplicate or mispaired courses.
  • Re-derives maximum marks, total credits, total credit points and the credit structure, then compares them with what the sheet prints.
  • Runs several hundred PDFs in one pass, keeps going in a background tab, and lists any file with no readable text layer.

02

Four checks in one toolkit

White vs Original Verification

A toolkit built around the white-background versus sealed-original comparison, with three related checks that use the same PDFs and TR. Each check produces its own numbered Excel report.

  • White ↔ original
  • SGPA
  • Percentage
  • Withheld
  • Failed
  • White vs original Pairs the two sets on registration number, then compares every identity field, course row, total, summary value and the publication date.
  • SGPA & percentage Recomputes percentage and SGPA from each sheet's own rows, along with row credit points, column totals and maximum marks.
  • Withheld students Reads the TR and lists students whose grace cell carries a W, exporting every grace cell with its Excel row number.
  • Failed candidates Pulls failed candidates from the PDFs, the TR, or both, and cross-checks the two lists to show who was found by only one source.

03

Consolidated transcript ↔ exact arithmetic

Consolidated Marksheet Checker Tool

Reads each consolidated transcript PDF, recomputes every SGPA, CGPA, credit, mark and percentage from the printed subject rows, and checks each figure against what the transcript claims — with exact fraction arithmetic, so a value either matches or it does not. Results come out as one Excel report.

  • PDF → recompute
  • SGPA
  • CGPA
  • Credits
  • Percentage
  • Excel report
  • Reads the two-column semester layout and rotated pages; transcripts with no text layer are listed separately.
  • Checks each semester's credit sum against its printed CREDITS line first, so a mis-read row is surfaced instead of silently accepted.
  • Recomputes SGPA from the subject rows and CGPA using the selected Old or New formula, rounded exactly as the transcript expects.
  • Re-derives each grade from marks, plus total credits, obtained and maximum marks and the percentage, and compares them with the printed values.
  • Checks the programme and specialization against the expected text and the year of passing, with an editable Old / New grade scale.
Open Consolidated Marksheet Checker →
04

Regular TR ↔ improvement TR

Provisional Result Tool

Loads the Regular TR and, where there was one, the Improvement / Supplementary TR for each semester, keeps the better attempt for every subject, and decides who is eligible for a provisional result. Everything comes out as one 8-sheet Excel workbook.

  • Regular ↔ improvement
  • Up to 8 semesters
  • Withheld
  • Provisional
  • Excel report
  • One Regular and one Improvement slot per semester; pick the right sheet from each file, or merge every program sheet.
  • For every subject the higher of the Regular and Improvement totals is kept, and a student passes only if no subject in any semester is still F.
  • A W in any Grace cell — any semester, any subject, either TR — withholds the result, even when the paper is declared P.
  • Lists the Final Result (Provisional), Withheld and Fail — No Provisional students, plus the extracted values with sheet and row numbers.

05

TR / Truscholar ↔ eligibility

Tr-Tru Eligibility Checker

Takes one result file per semester — a Truscholar marksheet or a TR, as .xlsx or .csv — matches students across the semesters and marks each one Eligible or Not Eligible. A student is eligible only when no subject in any semester has the grade F or Fail.

  • Truscholar
  • TR
  • Up to 8 semesters
  • Eligible
  • Excel report
  • File type is auto-detected per semester, with a manual Truscholar / TR override.
  • Students are matched by Enrollment No, falling back to hall ticket number and then email.
  • A missing record or a semester with no letter grades makes the student Not Eligible; blank grades are listed as warnings.
  • Report: Summary, Eligible, Not Eligible, one sheet per semester, Warnings and How Decided.
Open Tr-Tru Eligibility Checker →
06

Gradesheet ↔ TR, plus improvement / supplementary re-check

Marksheet vs TR + Improvement Re-check

The Marksheet vs TR verification with a second page added. Run the main check on Page 1 as usual; Page 2 then takes an improvement or supplementary TR and re-checks only the students whose marks did not match because they re-appeared.

  • Page 1 · Main check
  • Page 2 · Improvement / Supplementary
  • Best-of rule
  • SGPA
  • Excel report
  • Page 1 is the same Marksheet vs TR verification; Page 2 works on its result and lists every student with a marks, grade, total, SGPA or percentage mismatch.
  • The new TR may hold only the re-appeared subjects and only a few students. The exam type (Improvement or Supplementary) is read from the file, or set by hand.
  • Choose whether the new TR marks replace the old ones, or the higher of the two is kept. Only subjects present in the new TR are replaced.
  • Total, percentage, credit points and SGPA are recomputed from both TRs combined. The report is the Page 1 report with the updated marks plus one Improvement-Supp Field Check sheet.

03 How it works

From sign-in to report

  1. 01

    Sign in

    Use the ID issued to the examination office team. Your session stays open in this browser until you sign out or it expires.

  2. 02

    Pick the tool

    Marksheet vs TR for gradesheets against the tabulated result; White vs Original for copy-to-copy comparison and the SGPA, withheld and failed checks; Consolidated Marksheet Checker to audit the consolidated transcript; Provisional Result to combine regular and improvement TRs; Tr-Tru Eligibility to check that no semester has an F; Marksheet vs TR + Improvement Re-check to re-check students who re-appeared in an improvement or supplementary exam.

  3. 03

    Load the files and run

    Add the TR workbook and the PDFs inside the tool, choose the format options if auto-detection needs a correction, then run the check.

  4. 04

    Download and come back

    Save the Excel report, then return to this page whenever you need another tool. The six applications keep their own state.

04 About

One door, six independent tools

This portal holds no verification logic of its own. It signs you in, explains what each tool does, and links to the six applications, which are maintained as separate projects and can be updated independently of this page.

All six tools are built to run in the browser: files are read by the page you open them in, and the reports are generated on your machine. This portal itself never receives a file. If you need this behaviour confirmed for a compliance record, verify it against the source of each application rather than relying on this summary.

Handling of documents

  • Sign-in here controls access to the portal page only. It does not encrypt or protect the documents you work with.
  • Downloaded reports are ordinary Excel files. Store them where your office normally keeps examination records.
  • Sign out when you are finished on a shared computer, and avoid leaving reports in a shared downloads folder.
  • Report anything that looks wrong in a result to the examination office before acting on it.
